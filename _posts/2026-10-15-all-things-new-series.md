Why should I give Jesus a chance? What could He possibly offer my life? And what would it actually mean to follow Him? Over three evenings, we’ll explore who He is, what He offers us, and what it looks like to live a life shaped by Him. Wherever you are in your journey, come with your questions and take a closer look at Him.

Pastor Ray Moaga is joining us from Australia, he will be our guest speaker for the series.

Mark your calendars, bring a friend, and we’ll see you this October!

🗓️ Thu-Sat, Oct 15-17
🕰️ 7.30pm-9pm (Dinner provided at 6.30pm)
📍 798 Thomson Road, S298186 | Level 1 Auditorium
🎟 Free of charge!
